# Changelog

## 1.0.0

- First version: your 1Password logins as a column, through op and the 1Password app. ↵ pastes the password, ⇧↵ the username, and ⌘C copies the username. A second column, *1Password codes*, pastes an item's current one-time code.
- Settings for the account, when you have several, and for listing one vault only.
